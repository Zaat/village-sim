Play. This is the first version you can play, not only watch. A **Watch / Play** switch sits at the bottom of the map: Watch is the village on its own, exactly as before; Play lets you take the lead as much or as little as you like.

**Orders.** Click a villager (Shift-click or Shift-drag a box for more) and right-click a tree, rock, berry bush, building site or damaged building to send them there. On a phone: tap the place, then press the order button in the inspector, or press and hold for a menu. A plain click or tap only ever selects and inspects, so nothing is ordered by accident. A command bar gives the common jobs (wood, stone, berries, farm, hunt, fish, a fixed job, **Free** to hand them back).

**Build, Road, Zone.** Pick a building and a green or red footprint follows the pointer and says why a spot will not do; click to mark a site and builders pick it up first. Drag to lay roads. Paint **zones** (homes, farms, work, civic) and the village builds its own buildings inside them when there is room.

**Research and the village plans.** Choose what the village studies. Three switches (Buildings, Research, Jobs) say what the village still plans for itself; amber means you are in charge of that part. The **Crowd** panel sets how the townsfolk who are not individually in play split their work between food, wood and stone.

**Longer lives.** Villagers live about 8 to 12 years instead of about 4, and childhood lasts a little over a year. Births slow as a town grows and each family can only have three young children at a time, so the population still climbs smoothly to a plateau, as before.

**Game over.** A village that empties, or stays below five people for a whole month (after its first year and a half), ends. The end screen tells what the village was: how long it lasted, its largest size, its era and its population over time. You can keep watching, start the same world again, or begin a new one.

**Menu, manual and About.** A ☰ menu (Resume, New world, Save, Load, Settings, How to play, About), a manual covering mouse and touch, an About card with the version, what is new, links and a **Report a problem** button that copies a diagnostics report, and a first-visit welcome.

**Fixes.** The Stats tab now counts the crowd's births and deaths. The village planners (paths, hamlets, bridges, upgrades, cabins) respect the Buildings switch.

**Tests.** New suites `play_events` (43 checks), `gameover_events` and the page tests `ui_play`, `ui_over` and `ui_help`.
