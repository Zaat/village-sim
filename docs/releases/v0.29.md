Villagers now think, dream and talk. About thirty kinds of thought come from their real state: hunger, mood, work, family, grief, a coming baby, the winter stores, festivals, discoveries, expeditions and material finds, rumours, plans, quarrels, money and old age. Sleepers dream, and two villagers who stop to chat speak and answer.

Bubbles scale with speed: full lines at 1&times; and below, a few words at 3&times;, a glyph at 10&times; and 30&times; (three at a time), none at 100&times;. Click a bubble to pin the line; the inspector has a **Mind** block, and the Events tab has **Talk of the village** (a live feed at slow speeds, daily summaries at all speeds). Claude-written lines are optional and off by default.

Thoughts use no random numbers, so the village runs exactly as before.
