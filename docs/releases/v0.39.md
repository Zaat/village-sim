# v0.39 — Late ages and language

- **Two new ages**: Atomic and Information, 39 new technologies (148 in all, eleven ages), a much longer farm-yield ladder, high-rise (32 beds) and arcology (64 beds) homes.
- **Spanish**: starts in Spanish when your browser is, language switch in Settings (English or Español), remembered. Main screens are translated; more follows.
- **Full screen**: Menu > Full screen, or the X key (not on iPhone Safari).
- **Fairer early game**: a study of the Settings switches found survival is the main brake on growth; harsh winters, droughts, fevers and fires are now less harsh on small villages, survival is a little gentler, Settings tags how hard each switch is, and a village down to a handful of people gets a warning and more travellers when it has room and food.

Play it: https://zaat.github.io/village-sim/
