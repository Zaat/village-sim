Insight, look and sound. This is the first public release. Villages now keep growing instead of stalling around 250 people, the game has generated music and a cleaner look, and it can measure itself.

**Growth.** Couples now try for children every day instead of only when they happen to chat, single young adults pair up (never close kin), and births follow the beds and food the village really has. In seven 30-year test worlds (island, continent, archipelago, lakeland; Gentle, Normal and Harsh) every village survived and most reached 450 to 650 people. A boom generation still causes a dip when it ages (up to about 16 percent), which is natural.

**Hardship.** Hardship now also changes the harvest: Gentle yields a little more, Harsh about a fifth less. Gentle is kind, Normal is balanced, Harsh is a real struggle that good play can survive. There is no hard game over beyond the existing empty-village rule. In the test worlds Harsh grows more slowly in the first ten years than Normal and Gentle.

**Music.** Tap the speaker (or press M) for generated stereo music (Web Audio, no files) that follows the village: season, mood, danger. It never changes quickly at high speed, but a battle or a disaster stirs it. Like and skip buttons in the speaker panel.

**Look and controls.** Icons and labels on the build palette (compact cards so the menu no longer covers the map), a Max speed preset (key 6) with a days-per-second readout, and touch building: tap to put the footprint down, drag to move it, press the check to confirm, with an Undo for a few seconds afterwards. Scenarios and Load are in the Menu. A game-over screen tells the best tales of your village.

**Fixes.** Lumber camps are now found for far-away trees (#172), and faith and openness can live in the same person (#173, only a soft pull remains).

**Insight tools.** `tools/coverage.js` plays whole worlds headless and counts which events and concerns ever happen, `tools/covbatch.sh` runs several at once, `tools/devstats.js` records development time and test time per release (charts in `docs/dev-stats.md`), and a growth limits ledger shows why a village stopped growing. The results of this release are in `docs/coverage.md`.

**Tests.** 32 suites (about 11 minutes, down from about 16) plus 15 page tests. New suite `growth_events` (11 checks). `stats_events` and `bld` now start from saved checkpoints, and the page tests caught two layout problems before release (top bar overlap and a tall build menu), both fixed.
