Materials are now rare and tied to discoveries: one deposit per material per map, recognised only once the village knows the matching craft (masonry for saltpetre, mining for coal, bronze for copper, steam for oil); neighbours sell a material only when friendly.

New places appear over time: diving bells reveal deep wrecks, sonar the sea bed, aviation a lost city, and a deep drought can uncover a hoard.

The new-world dialog has a **Check this world** button that lists a map's special features (barges, deposits, neighbours, hidden places).

The probe prints a yearly population flow (births, deaths by cause, arrivals, outpost population) to find out why some seeds dip around years 7&ndash;14.
