# v0.39.3 — Events, performance and polish

- **Events tab, redesigned**: *Now* (what the village is doing), a ranked *Events* feed with chips, and *Years* with decades. Events that happened somewhere have a pin that shows you where.
- **A living crowd**: the townsfolk beyond the villagers in play walk to work, work, meet at the plaza in the evening and go home to sleep. Drawing only; it costs 0 to 4 ms a frame in the largest village we have.
- **Select boats, ships, wagons, trees and rocks.**
- **A clearer build panel** in Play, and more of the game in Spanish.
- **The same seed now gives the same village in every browser.** Six random sorts that made Node and Chromium disagree from about day 12 were replaced by a fair shuffle. The golden file was regenerated; a seed started again can give a different village than before. Saves are not affected.
- **Diagnostics** check what sits on top of the tabs, no longer read 0 for step time, and say whether the browser keeps our storage.
- **Tests and CI**: a GitHub Actions workflow, a cross-engine check, and new suites for the event record, determinism and unity after a secession.
- Moved to v0.40: smoother heavy ticks and the simulation's own path caches (they change results and need their own review).
