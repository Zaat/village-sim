# Glossary

Words the game and these documents use in their own way.

**Age.** A stage of technology, from the Stone Age to the Information Age (eleven in all). The village enters an age when enough of its technologies are known. Not the same as a villager's age.

**Ambitions.** A planned list of next great works and goals with visible rewards (see the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md)).

**Clean layout.** A view with the side panel and most controls hidden. Key L, or the corner button, returns to Docked.

**Concern.** Something the village notices about its own numbers (food running low, not enough homes) and raises as a badge, with responses and a state.

**Council.** The elected group (Chief, Warden, Steward, Speaker, councillors) that meets every four days, votes on policy and picks what to research.

**Crowd.** The residents beyond the 160 simulated one by one. They are kept as counts by age and by kind of work, follow the same food, housing and work rules, and are drawn as small figures at their homes.

**Culture.** A score from 0 to 100 built from weddings, funerals and story nights. See [DESIGN](DESIGN.md) entry 8.

**Docked layout.** The default: the side panel beside the map.

**Focus villagers.** The up to 160 people simulated in full, with needs, routes, relationships and stories. They are the ones you can click on and follow.

**Golden file.** A recording of the exact outcome of reference worlds. A release that changes it must say why.

**Hardship.** The difficulty setting: Gentle, Normal or Harsh, chosen at world creation and changeable live.

**Hamlet.** A satellite cluster of homes that the village founds when the close ground fills up.

**Milestone.** On GitHub, a named group of issues. This project has one per release, plus planned ones.

**Outpost.** A settlement founded by the village (logging, mining, farming, trade) that can grow loyal or restless.

**Pace lab.** A developer tool that compares population growth under different birth models.

**Play and Watch.** Two ways to run the same village. Watch is fully automatic; in Play you can give orders, build, lay roads and choose research.

**Regime.** How the village is ruled, read off six axes (authority, community, faith, industry, openness, unity) and given a label.

**Risk switches.** Four Settings that turn the harder parts on or off: survival, disasters, crime and raids.

**Seed.** The number that fixes a world. The same seed, map and settings give the same village.

**Tick.** The simulation's smallest step. A day is 1,200 ticks; 1× speed is 20 ticks a second; a year is 32 days.

**World line.** One line of text (version, seed, map, size, hardship, switches, date) that can be copied from Settings and pasted into the Seed box to rebuild a world.
